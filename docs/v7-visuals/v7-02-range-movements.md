**Files:** `v7_rom_<movement>.png` · 1536×1024 · warm white background
**Where they are used:** the picture box at the top of each movement's setup screen (see `references/screen_range_setup_ar.png`), above the four short instructions. The app mirrors each picture when the left side is tested.
**Attach:** the style set, palette.png and the cast sheet with Fahd.

Every picture follows the v7 addendum:
- the RIGHT side moves;
- the start pose is a faint ghost and the end pose is solid, with one gold arrow;
- the phone is in the scene at the stated distance and height;
- the effort is calm and comfortable.

The movements, positions and phone placement come from Azm's signed-off range of motion protocol (4 October 2026), so keep them exactly as written.
