# V7-03 · Walk setup pictures

**Files:** `v7_walk_<name>.png` · 1536×1024 · warm white background
**Where they are used:** the picture box at the top of each walk setup screen. Today these boxes hold simple top-down plans; see `references/screen_walk_*.png`. The app mirrors the side view pictures when the phone goes on the left.
**Attach:** the style set, palette.png and the cast sheet with Fahd.

These pictures show **how to set up**, so they have no ghost and no arrow unless a prompt asks for one. The phone's orientation matters:
- **sideways** (landscape) for side views;
- **upright** (portrait) for front views.

## Prompts

**01 · v7_walk_pad_side.png** (the walking pad, phone at the side)
Make walk setup picture 01. Fahd walks at an easy pace on the walking pad, holding the front handle bar with his left hand. The phone is turned sideways (landscape) on its slim tripod at his hip height, about 3 metres from the pad on his right side, facing the middle of the belt. His helper, Sara, stands just behind him on the phone's side, within arm's reach of him and close to the red stop button. The room is calm and nearly empty, with clear floor space behind the pad. Three quarter view from slightly above, the pad, Fahd, Sara and the phone all in the frame. Landscape 1536 by 1024, warm white, no text, no numbers on the pad.
Reject if: the phone is upright, it stands in front instead of to the side, the helper is missing or far away, there is no clear space behind the pad, or the pad shows a screen with numbers.

**02 · v7_walk_pad_front.png** (the walking pad, phone in front)
Make walk setup picture 02. Fahd walks at an easy pace on the walking pad, holding the front handle bar. The phone stands upright (portrait) on its tripod about 1 metre high, 2 metres in front of the pad, in line with the belt and facing him. Sara stands just behind him, close to the red stop button. Three quarter view from slightly above and to the side, so the pad, the phone in front of it, Fahd and Sara all show. Landscape 1536 by 1024, warm white, no text.
Reject if: the phone is sideways or off to the side, or the helper is missing.

**03 · v7_walk_side_path.png** (normal walking, phone at the side)
Make walk setup picture 03. A calm, plain room or hallway with a clear straight walking path about 6 metres long, marked at each end by a small strip of gold tape on the floor beyond the phone's view. Fahd walks across the path from left to right, steadily and without an aid. The phone is turned sideways (landscape) on its tripod at his hip height, about 3.5 metres from the path, facing the middle of the path. Add one faint gold dashed line on the floor showing his route there and back. Three quarter view from slightly above and behind the phone, so the phone, the path and Fahd all show. Landscape 1536 by 1024, warm white, no text.
Reject if: the phone is upright, it stands in the path, there are obstacles or rugs on the path, or Fahd uses a walking aid.

**04 · v7_walk_front_path.png** (normal walking, phone at the end of the path)
Make walk setup picture 04. A calm, plain room or hallway with a clear straight path. The phone stands upright (portrait) on its tripod at hip height at the end of the path, about half a metre to the side of the path, not in it, facing back along the path. A small gold tape start mark lies at the far end, and a stop mark 1.5 metres behind the phone. Fahd walks along the path toward the phone. Three quarter view from slightly above and to the side. Landscape 1536 by 1024, warm white, no text.
Reject if: the phone stands in the middle of the path, the marks are missing, or the path is cluttered.

**05 · v7_walk_pad_safety.png** (before stepping on the pad)
Make walk setup picture 05. The walking pad stands on a level floor with clear space behind it. The belt is stopped. Fahd stands beside the pad, ready to step on, his left hand on the front handle bar. Sara stands beside the pad with one hand near the red stop button, looking at Fahd calmly. No phone in this picture. Three quarter view from slightly above. Landscape 1536 by 1024, warm white, no text.
Reject if: the belt looks like it is moving, Fahd is already walking, the helper is not near the stop button, or anything blocks the space behind the pad.
