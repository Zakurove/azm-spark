## Prompts

**01 · v7_landing_range.png** (the focused range of motion check)
Make landing picture 01. Fahd sits upright on the dining chair, his right side toward a phone that stands upright on its slim tripod about 2.5 metres away. He raises his right arm in front of him to a comfortable height, calm and focused. Behind his arm, draw one soft, wide translucent gold #F8CB44 arc that follows the path of his arm, like a gentle measuring arc, with no numbers and no ticks. Warm white #FBFAF8 background with a faint cream #FDF5DF glow behind him and a soft floor shadow. Keep the left third of the picture calm and nearly empty. Three quarter view from slightly above eye level. Landscape 1536 by 1024, no text.
Then make the phone crop: the same scene recomposed for 1024 by 1280 (portrait), Fahd and the arc centred, the phone still visible.
Reject if: the arc has numbers or ticks, the image looks clinical, or the phone is large and in front of him.

Behind him on the floor, three or four soft translucent gold footprints fade away, showing his steps. Warm white background with a faint cream glow and a soft floor shadow. Keep the left third calm. Landscape 1536 by 1024, no text.
Then make the phone crop for 1024 by 1280 (portrait), Fahd centred mid stride.
